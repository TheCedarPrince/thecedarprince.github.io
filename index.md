My name is Jacob Scott Zelko!
I am currently a [Software Engineer at PlantingSpace](https://planting.space) and [Research Affiliate at University of Saskatchewan's Computational Epidemiology and Public Health Informatics Lab (CEPHIL)](https://cephil.ca)
Additionally, I am the leader of the open source and open science community, [JuliaHealth](juliahealth.org).

My interests lie at the intersection of population health dynamics, applied category theory, knowledge representation, and open source.
In particular, I am interested in how we can apply relational thinking approaches from pure mathematics to establish meaningful relationships between non-traditional health data sources to gain greater insights into population health.
I have been actively involved within the [OHDSI Community](https://www.ohdsi.org), [Category Theory Zulip](https://johncarlosbaez.wordpress.com/2020/03/25/category-theory-community-server/) , and [Julia Programming Language Slack](https://julialang.org/slack/).

In the past, I worked for a couple years at [Georgia Tech Research Institute](https://gtri.gatech.edu/) in the excellent HEAT Division as a Health Data Analytics and Informatics Researcher headed by [Dr. Jon Duke](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/people/jon-duke) and as a Research Engineer for the [Centers for Disease Control](https://www.cdc.gov/).
While in my undergrad, I also worked at [Emory University's Department of Biomedical Informatics](https://med.emory.edu/departments/biomedical-informatics/index.html) as a Data Information Specialist.
I graduated from [Northeastern University](https://cos.northeastern.edu/nucos-department/mathematics/) with an MS in Applied Mathematics, [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.bme.gatech.edu/) with a BS in Biomedical Engineering [also completed coursework in biomedical informatics and medical anthropology], and an AS in Engineering Science at [Corning Community College](https://www.corning-cc.edu/academics/engineering-science/index.php).
